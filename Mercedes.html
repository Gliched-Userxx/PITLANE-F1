Mercedes AMG Petronas

W17


The Mercedes-AMG F1 W17 E Performance, commonly known as the W17, is the Formula One racing car designed by the Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team for the 2026 Formula One World Championship. It is the first car built under F1's major 2026 technical regulation reset, featuring significant changes to the chassis, power unit, and fuel.

DRIVERS

GEORGE RUSSEL

George Russell is a British Formula 1 driver competing for Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team. Russell joined Formula 1 in 2019 with Williams after winning the Formula 2 championship. Despite driving an uncompetitive car during his early seasons, he impressed the paddock with his consistency, speed, and leadership qualities. Russell moved to Mercedes in 2022 and gradually became the team leader. He is known for his precise driving style, excellent racecraft, and ability to extract maximum performance from the car. By the 2026 season he is considered one of the strongest championship contenders and continues to lead Mercedes into the new era of Formula 1 regulations.

KIMI ANTONELLI

Andrea Kimi Antonelli is an Italian Formula 1 driver racing for Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team. He is widely regarded as one of the most talented young drivers in modern motorsport. Antonelli dominated several junior racing series before reaching Formula 1, where his natural speed and fearless driving style quickly impressed fans and experts. Even at a young age he showed remarkable maturity behind the wheel, strong overtaking ability, and excellent qualifying performances. Antonelli represents the new generation of Formula 1 drivers and is expected to compete for race wins and championships in the future.
