The VCARB 02 was the Formula 1 car fielded by the Visa Cash App Racing Bulls during the 2025 World Championship. Representing the final evolution of the ground-effect technical era before the major 2026 regulation overhaul, it was powered by the Honda RBPTH003 1.6L V6 turbo hybrid engine. Architecturally, the car featured a carbon-fibre monocoque and a pull-rod front suspension design heavily influenced by its sister team, Red Bull Racing. Visually, the VCARB 02 was notable for its striking liveries, including a special "street art" collaboration with artist Slawn for the 2025 British Grand Prix.

LIAM LAWSON

Liam Lawson is a New Zealand Formula 1 driver competing for RB Formula One Team. Lawson first gained international attention when he stepped in as a substitute driver during a Formula 1 race weekend and delivered an impressive performance despite having very limited preparation time. Before reaching Formula 1 he achieved strong results in junior championships including Formula 2 and various international racing series. Lawson is known for his fearless overtaking style, aggressive race pace, and determination to prove himself at the highest level of motorsport. His promotion to a full-time Formula 1 seat reflects the confidence that his team has in his abilities. As he gains more experience, Lawson is expected to become one of the most competitive young drivers on the grid.
