SCUDERIA FERRARI

SF-26


The Ferrari SF-26, also known by its internal name Project 678, is a Formula One car constructed by Scuderia Ferrari to compete in the 2026 Formula One World Championship. It is currently being driven by Charles Leclerc and seven-time World Drivers' Champion Lewis Hamilton in their eighth and second season with Ferrari, respectively.

DRIVERS

LEWIS HAMILTON

Lewis Hamilton is a British racing legend competing for Scuderia Ferrari in 2026. Hamilton is one of the most successful drivers in Formula 1 history, with numerous race wins, pole positions, and world championships. He originally debuted with McLaren in 2007 before achieving huge success with Mercedes. His move to Ferrari marked one of the most dramatic transfers in Formula 1 history. Hamilton is admired for his incredible speed, race intelligence, and ability to perform consistently at the highest level even after many years in the sport.

CHARLES LECLEREC

Charles Leclerc is a Monégasque driver racing for Scuderia Ferrari. Leclerc is famous for his outstanding qualifying pace and ability to push the car to its absolute limits. He joined Ferrari in 2019 and quickly became the team's leading driver. Over the years he has achieved multiple race victories and pole positions. Known for his calm personality and technical understanding of the car, Leclerc remains Ferrari's key driver in their pursuit of another world championship.
