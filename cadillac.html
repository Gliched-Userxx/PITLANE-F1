Cadillac F1

MAC-26


The Cadillac MAC-26 is the inaugural Formula One car designed and constructed by the Cadillac Formula 1 Team for the 2026 season. The chassis name, MAC-26, stands for Mario Andretti Cadillac 26, serving as a tribute to 1978 World Champion Mario Andretti and his pivotal role in bringing the 11th team to the grid.

DRIVERS

VALTTERI BOTTAS

Valtteri Bottas is a Finnish Formula 1 driver racing for the Cadillac Formula One Team. Bottas began his Formula 1 career with Williams Racing before moving to Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula One Team, where he became one of the most consistent drivers on the grid. During his time at Mercedes he achieved several race victories and helped the team win multiple Constructors Championships. Bottas is known for his smooth driving style, strong qualifying pace, and ability to provide valuable technical feedback to engineers. His experience makes him an ideal driver for Cadillac as the team builds its Formula 1 project from the ground up. In the 2026 season he aims to help develop the car while also competing for strong race results whenever opportunities arise.

SERGIO CHECO PEREZ

Sergio Pérez is a Mexican Formula 1 driver competing for the new Cadillac Formula One Team in the 2026 season. Pérez has built a long and successful career in Formula 1, racing for teams such as Sauber, Force India, Racing Point, and Red Bull Racing. He is widely known for his exceptional tyre management skills, which allow him to extend tyre life longer than many other drivers. This ability earned him the nickname “King of Street Circuits.” Pérez has achieved multiple race victories and numerous podium finishes during his career. As Cadillac enters Formula 1, his experience, leadership, and racecraft will play a crucial role in helping the team establish itself in the championship.
