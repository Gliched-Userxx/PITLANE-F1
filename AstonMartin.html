Aramco Aston Martin

AMR26


The AMR26 is the 2026 Formula One car from the Aston Martin Aramco F1 Team, marking a major transition as the team's first "works" entry in partnership with Honda. It is also the first Aston Martin challenger designed under the leadership of Adrian Newey, who joined as Managing Technical Partner and Team Principal.

DRIVERS

FERNANDO ALONSO

Fernando Alonso is one of the most experienced and respected drivers in the history of Formula 1. The Spanish racing driver currently competes for Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team in the 2026 season. Alonso first entered Formula 1 in 2001 and quickly built a reputation for his extraordinary racecraft, strategic thinking, and ability to extract maximum performance from any car he drives. He became a two-time World Champion in 2005 and 2006 while racing for Renault F1 Team, ending the long championship dominance of Michael Schumacher. Throughout his long career he has raced for several top teams including McLaren and Scuderia Ferrari.

LANCE STROLL

Lance Stroll is a Canadian Formula 1 driver who races alongside Alonso at Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team. Stroll made his Formula 1 debut in 2017 with Williams Racing and immediately made history by becoming one of the youngest drivers to start a race in the sport. During his rookie season he achieved an impressive podium finish at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix, showcasing his ability to perform under challenging conditions. Stroll is particularly known for his strong performances in wet races, where his car control and confidence allow him to compete against more experienced drivers. Over the years he has steadily improved his race pace, tyre management, and overall consistency.
