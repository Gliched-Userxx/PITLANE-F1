MCL-40


The McLaren MCL40 is McLaren's entry for the 2026 Formula One World Championship, designed under the technical leadership of Rob Marshall. This car marks a new era for the team as they defend their 2025 Constructors' and Drivers' World Championship titles.The MCL40 was built to address the radical 2026 regulation changes, featuring a "clean sheet" design that is smaller and lighter than its predecessor.

DRIVERS

OSCAR PIASTRI

Oscar Piastri is an Australian Formula 1 driver racing alongside Norris at McLaren. Before reaching Formula 1, Piastri achieved one of the most impressive junior careers in motorsport history by winning the Formula Renault Eurocup, Formula 3 Championship, and Formula 2 Championship in consecutive seasons. He joined Formula 1 in 2023 and quickly demonstrated his potential with podium finishes and strong race performances. Piastri is known for his calm personality, intelligent race management, and remarkable consistency. His technical feedback also helps engineers improve the car throughout the season.

LANDO NORRIS

Lando Norris is a British racing driver competing for McLaren and the reigning Formula 1 World Champion heading into the 2026 season. Norris made his Formula 1 debut in 2019 after a successful junior career where he won multiple championships in karting and junior single-seater categories. Over the years he developed into one of the fastest and most consistent drivers on the grid, known for his smooth driving style, exceptional qualifying speed, and ability to manage tyres effectively during races. His breakthrough victory came at the 2024 Miami Grand Prix, and he continued improving until he eventually secured the Drivers Championship in 2025. Norris is also extremely popular among fans due to his humor, gaming streams, and active presence on social media.
