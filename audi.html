Audi F1

R26


The Audi R26 represents a historic shift in Formula 1, marking the Four Rings' first-ever entry as a full factory squad. Developed across two high-tech hubs—Hinwil, Switzerland, for the chassis and Neuburg, Germany, for the power unit—the R26 is a response to the massive 2026 regulation overhaul. At its heart is the Audi AFR 26 Hybrid, a 1.6-litre V6 turbo that achieves a near-perfect 50/50 power split between its internal combustion engine and a massive 350kW electric motor, totaling roughly 1,000 hp. To manage this power, the car features radical active aerodynamics, including moveable front and rear wings designed to reduce drag on straights and maximize downforce in corners.

DRIVERS

NICO HULKENBERG

Nico Hülkenberg is a German Formula 1 driver competing for the Audi F1 Team. Known for his exceptional qualifying performances and technical understanding of race cars, Hülkenberg has built a long and respected career in Formula 1. Before joining Audi, he raced for several teams including Renault F1 Team, Force India, and Haas F1 Team. Although he has often driven midfield cars, Hülkenberg has consistently demonstrated his ability to score points and deliver strong race performances. His experience and calm approach make him a valuable asset for Audi as the manufacturer builds its new Formula 1 program. Hülkenberg's role involves not only racing but also helping engineers develop the car and adapt to the new regulations introduced in 2026.

GABRIEL BORTOLETO

Gabriel Bortoleto is a Brazilian Formula 1 driver racing for the Audi F1 Team. Bortoleto rose through the junior motorsport ladder with remarkable success, winning championships and demonstrating exceptional talent from an early stage of his career. His smooth driving style, excellent tyre management, and strong qualifying performances quickly attracted attention from Formula 1 teams. As one of the few Brazilian drivers to reach Formula 1 in recent years, Bortoleto carries the hopes of many fans from a country with a rich motorsport heritage that includes legends such as Ayrton Senna. Joining Audi during the team's early years in Formula 1 gives him the opportunity to grow alongside the team while developing into a future race-winning driver.
