A526


The Alpine A526 is the BWT Alpine F1 Team's challenger for the 2026 Formula 1 World Championship. It marks a significant turning point for the team as it transitions from being a Renault "works" team to a Mercedes customer.Since the Alpine A526 is the 2026 car, "him" likely refers to the team's rising star, Franco Colapinto, who partners with the experienced Pierre Gasly for the 2026 F1 Season.

DRIVERS

PIERRE GASLY

Pierre Gasly is a French Formula 1 driver racing for Alpine F1 Team. Gasly began his Formula 1 career with teams associated with the Red Bull development program and gradually built a reputation as a highly skilled and determined driver. One of the most memorable moments of his career came at the 2020 Italian Grand Prix, where he achieved an emotional and unexpected victory after a dramatic race. Gasly is known for his resilience and ability to bounce back from difficult situations. His driving style combines aggression with precision, allowing him to compete strongly even when driving a midfield car. At Alpine he plays an important role in helping the team improve its performance through technical feedback and consistent race results.

FRANCO COLAPINTO

Franco Colapinto is an Argentine Formula 1 driver who competes for Alpine F1 Team. He represents a new generation of talented young drivers entering the sport. Colapinto gained recognition through impressive results in various junior single-seater championships where he demonstrated excellent race pace and aggressive overtaking abilities. His strong performances attracted the attention of Formula 1 teams, eventually earning him a seat with Alpine. Colapinto is known for his determination, fearless racing style, and strong adaptability to different racing conditions. As one of the few Argentine drivers to reach Formula 1 in recent decades, he has gained significant support from fans around the world.
