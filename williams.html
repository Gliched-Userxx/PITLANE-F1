Williams Racing

FW48


The Williams FW48 has faced a challenging start to the 2026 season, marked by technical setbacks and performance struggles during the opening rounds. Despite high expectations following the team's fifth-place finish in 2025, the car has yet to score points as of the 2026 Chinese Grand Prix.Williams prioritised FW48 development by ending the 2025 car's cycle early. However, production delays and reportedly failing three FIA crash tests caused the team to miss the initial Barcelona shakedown in late January.

DRIVERS

CARLOS SAINZ

Carlos Sainz Jr. is a Spanish driver racing for Williams Racing in the 2026 season. Sainz began his Formula 1 career with Toro Rosso and later raced for Renault, McLaren, and Ferrari. He is widely respected for his intelligence, consistency, and ability to deliver strong results in difficult races. His technical feedback and experience are extremely valuable for teams developing their cars. At Williams, he plays an important role in helping the team return to competitiveness.

ALEXENDER ALBON

Alexander Albon is a Thai-British Formula 1 driver racing alongside Sainz at Williams Racing. Albon previously drove for Red Bull Racing before rebuilding his career with Williams. He is known for his smooth driving style, strategic thinking, and ability to extract strong performances from midfield cars. Albon has become the leader of Williams driver lineup and consistently scores important points for the team.
